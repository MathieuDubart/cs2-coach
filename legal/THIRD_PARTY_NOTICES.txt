WILY - THIRD-PARTY NOTICES
==========================

Wily is a free CS2 coaching app made by Valentin Gassend (https://wily.valentingassend.com). It is not affiliated with,
endorsed or sponsored by Valve Corporation. Counter-Strike, Counter-Strike 2, CS2 and Steam are trademarks of Valve
Corporation.

The app uses only what Valve provides for that: Game State Integration (the game's official feature for HUDs and
RGB keyboards), the console log the game writes when launched with -condebug, the Steam Web API with your own key,
and the demos of your own matches. It never reads the game's memory or network traffic, and the training server is a
separate local copy of the game that only you join.

DATA AND IMAGES
---------------

Map radar images: Valve's radars as published by CS Demo Manager (https://github.com/akiver/cs-demo-manager,
  MIT, LICENSES/cs-demo-manager-LICENSE.txt). The images are Valve's.

The base (out/population, out/style, train_*): computed by Wily's author from matchmaking demos. Every player in it has a
  made-up SteamID and no name, except your own matches and the public pro matches.

Pro matches (out/deep_pro, out/style_pro, out/population/*_pro, pros.json): extracted from the public demos of
  professional matches hosted by HLTV.org (https://www.hltv.org). Player and team names as published there.

Lineup book of Fachwerk: by gronnmann (https://github.com/gronnmann/de-fachwerk-smokes). Not shipped: the app
  downloads it from its author's repository and shows it with its author's name and a link.

Lineup catalog of Daily CS Nades (https://dailycsnades.com/data/cs2-pro-utility-dataset): not shipped until its
  authors agree. Daily CS Nades (2026). Daily CS Nades Professional CS2 Utility Dataset.


DOWNLOADED BY THE APP ON YOUR PC, WHEN YOU USE THE FEATURE
---------------------------------------------------------

Your matches' demos: from Valve's servers, through your Steam client.
Speech recognition model (optional, your calls): Systran's faster-whisper models on Hugging Face (MIT).
Player statistics: Leetify's public API (https://leetify.com), for your own profile and the players you search.
Your Steam profile (name, avatar): Steam Community.
